1. How the school is organised

  • A TRACK is the curriculum family. The school has one: TVL (Technical-Vocational-Livelihood).
  • A STRAND is the specialisation a Senior High student takes in that track. There are 10.
  • A COURSE is the programme a Bundled student takes for the one or two years of the TESDA track. There are 3.
  • A SECTION is a class in one year level, and it points at the strand or course it teaches.

The year levels in use: 1st Year, 2nd Year, Grade 11, Grade 12.

7. How an enrolment sheet is read

  • A strand is matched by its name, its code or any of its aliases - the school sheet may spell it in the official DepEd / TESDA wording.
  • A course is matched the same way, by name, code or alias.
  • A cluster or programme-group name (Industrial Technologies, Home Economics, Agri-Fishery Arts, ICT) is not a strand. The student inherits the strand of their SECTION, and the importer says so in its report (strands_inherited_from_section).
  • A section is the authority: when a student is moved to a section, they take that section's strand and year level with them.
  • A Senior High row carries the strand; a Bundled row carries the course.
  • Students are matched by LRN, so re-importing the same file updates the students instead of creating duplicates.
  • The sheet's own wording for the track is kept as text; the strand itself comes from the record above.

8. Reference notes

  • The school is a private Senior High institution - DepEd school id 406005 - and is 100 % TVL in the current school year, which matches the data: one track (TVL), ten strands, and every Senior High section a TVL specialisation.
  • The six TESDA-registered programmes that map onto the school's strands are SMAW, Computer Systems Servicing, Electronic Products Assembly and Servicing, Food and Beverage Services, Housekeeping and Organic Agriculture Production.
  • The three Bundled courses use the 2024 revised TESDA qualification titles: Automotive Mechanic, Computer and Electronics, and Hotel and Restaurant Services.
  • The strands are grouped under the four TVL strands: Industrial Arts (SMAW, EPAS, Automotive Servicing), ICT (ICT-CSS, ICT (.NET), ICT (JAVA)), Home Economics (FBS, HK, Bartending) and Agri-Fishery Arts (ACP).
  • DepEd Memorandum No. 012, s. 2026 replaces the strand system with the Strengthened SHS Curriculum (two tracks: Academic and Technical-Professional, with electives instead of strands). The incoming Grade 11 falls under it; the Grade 12s stay on the old strands.

9. Notes on the data as it stands

  • 7 section(s) carry both a strand and a course: 1A, 2A, Esther, James, Proverbs, Thessalonians, Hebrews. The strand is the one that counts for a Senior High section.
  • One strand name is missing its closing bracket (“Automotive Servicing (NCI & NCII”). The aliases cover every spelling, so imports still land correctly.
  • Section advisers are filled in from School Config → Advisers.
  • Student counts are the current enrolment in each strand, course and section.
